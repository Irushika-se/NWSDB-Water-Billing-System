About NWSDB

The National Water Supply & Drainage Board (NWSDB) is responsible for providing water supply and drainage services across Sri Lanka.

The NWSDB Online Portal provides a convenient digital platform for customers to manage their water service information. Customers can view their profile, check current bills, monitor water usage, view payment history, and make simulated online bill payments.

NWSDB Staff use the system to manage customer information, generate bills, record meter readings, and monitor payment records. Approved Partners can access authorized services through the NWSDB API to support customer bill-related operations.

The portal is designed to provide a simple and organized way to manage customer, billing, water usage, and payment information while maintaining role-based access to the system.
